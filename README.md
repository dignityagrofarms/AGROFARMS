# Dignity Agro Website

Build a professional, modern website for Dignity Agro Farms Limited, a poultry farming business.

BRAND
- Name: Dignity Agro Farms Limited
- Tagline: "Rooted in Care. Driven by Purpose. Growing with Integrity."
- Industry: Poultry farming — broiler farming, layer farming, fresh egg supply, poultry production, and farm consultancy
- Brand colors: Dark forest green (#0F3D24) as primary, a fresh leafy green (#3F8F3F) as accent, with a warm off-white/cream (#F7F5F0) background. Use the greens for headers, buttons, and section dividers.
- Tone: warm, trustworthy, professional — not corporate-cold, not overly rustic. Think "modern farm brand," similar to how specialty food or agritech brands present themselves.
- Include a leaf motif subtly in section dividers or icons, echoing an organic/natural feel.

PAGES / STRUCTURE

1. HOME
- Hero section with a strong headline (e.g. "Poultry Farming Rooted in Care") and subheadline using the tagline
- A short intro paragraph about the farm's mission
- Visual row/icons for the five business lines: Broiler Farming, Layer Farming, Fresh Eggs Supply, Poultry Production, Farm Consultancy
- A "Why Dignity" section highlighting 3-4 pillars: Quality, Trust, Sustainability, Care
- Featured products preview (broilers, eggs) with a "View Products" CTA
- Testimonial/social proof placeholder section
- Newsletter or "Follow Our Journey" signup with Instagram/Facebook links
- Footer with contact info, social links (@dignityagrofarms), and quick nav links

2. ABOUT US
- "Our Beginning" story section (vision to raise poultry with care, integrity, and commitment to quality)
- Mission, Vision, and Core Values (Integrity, Care, Consistency, Growth) as a clean 4-card grid
- A simple team/structure section (Founder & Farm Manager leading operations)
- Farm photos gallery placeholder (grid layout, image placeholders)

3. PRODUCTS & SERVICES
- Separate sections/cards for: Broilers, Layers, Fresh Eggs, Poultry Production, Farm Consultancy
- Each with: description, ideal customer (retail/wholesale/household), and a "Request Order" or "Enquire" button
- Include a simple pricing/order note: "Contact us for current pricing and bulk order rates"

4. ORDER / CONTACT
- A clean order/inquiry form: Name, Phone, Email, Product Interested In (dropdown: Broilers, Layers, Eggs, Consultancy), Quantity, Delivery Location, Message
- Contact details section: phone, email, location/service area, business hours
- Embedded map placeholder for farm location
- Social links (Instagram, Facebook) with icons

5. BLOG / FARM UPDATES (optional simple version)
- A simple grid of blog/update cards (Farm Tips, Behind-the-Scenes, Announcements) with placeholder posts
- This should be easy to expand later with new posts

FUNCTIONALITY
- Fully responsive (mobile-first, since most Nigerian customers will visit via phone)
- Fast-loading, clean navigation bar with logo left, nav links center/right, and a prominent "Order Now" or "Contact Us" button
- Sticky header on scroll
- Smooth scroll animations between sections (subtle, not distracting)
- Working contact/order form that captures submissions (connect to an email notification or simple backend so inquiries aren't lost)
- WhatsApp click-to-chat button (floating, bottom-right) linking to a WhatsApp number for quick orders
- SEO-friendly structure: proper headings, meta title/description mentioning "poultry farm," "fresh eggs," "broiler and layer supply," and the farm's likely service location
- Social proof: follower/rating counters or testimonial carousel if feasible

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Professional and trustworthy, similar to a modern agribusiness or food brand site — not cluttered, generous white space, high-quality imagery placeholders for chickens/eggs/farm scenes
- Typography: a clean modern sans-serif for body text, and a slightly bolder/rounded font for headings to match the friendly "Dignity" logo style
- Use card-based layouts with soft shadows and rounded corners for product/service sections
- Include subtle green gradient or wave-shaped section dividers (matching the brand's existing marketing material style)
